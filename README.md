Little Guy, Big Desk

Theme: I'm just a little guy!

In this game, you are a tiny little person living on a very big desk!

Everything around you is HUGE! A coffee mug looks like a lake, pencils look like big walls, and a giant cat keeps trying to catch you!

Your job is to collect tiny pieces of food called crumbs and bring them back to your little matchbox home.

You need to collect 60 crumbs to build your own tiny castle!

But be careful! A big cat might hit you with its paw. If the cat catches you, you lose the crumbs you are carrying and go back home.

How to Play

WASD or arrow keys: Move around.

Z: See the whole big desk.

E: Talk to your ant friend, Bob.

Space: Make a funny squeaky sound.

Enter: Chat with friends.

1, 2, 3, 4: Make funny faces and emotes.

You can carry only 8 crumbs at once, so keep bringing them home!

Play With Friends

You can open the game in different tabs of the same browser and play with other tiny people!

You can talk, make funny faces, and see who collects the most crumbs.
